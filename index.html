The conversation

Where design
and innovation
go next.

Every leader in the room is being asked the same thing: what does your function look like when the tools can draft almost anything?

That question is too big for a panel and too interesting for a keynote. So we put a small group around one table — people running design, product, brand and innovation where the answer has consequences — and let the conversation go where it goes. Fable brings a sharp view of who gets designed for and who gets left out. DDX brings the room.
